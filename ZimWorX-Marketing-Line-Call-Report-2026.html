Calls by day and interval

Calls offered cluster between 8 AM and 4 PM CT. From 8 AM to 12:59 PM CT the team answers 38% to 52% of calls in each hour. The busiest hours are 1 PM and 2 PM CT, with 61 and 62 calls offered, and that is exactly where the rate falls: 33% at 1 PM, 18% at 2 PM and 11% at 3 PM. None of the 35 calls offered from 4 PM CT onward were answered.

Of the 55 calls answered while only the overflow team was on shift, 31 were picked up by Michelle and Rutendo after their own shift had ended. Opportunity: weekend coverage

The team does not work weekends. This year 12 genuine calls arrived on a Saturday or Sunday, US Central time, and all 12 went to voicemail.

12genuine weekend calls, 0 answered
6 and 6calls on Saturdays and Sundays

Those callers are likely to go elsewhere: 82% of US consumers say they will call a competitor when a business does not answer (CallRail 2025, via Missed Call Statistics 2026). A short Saturday shift, or a missed-call text-back on weekends, would capture them.

About 1 in 10 calls is spam

Spam falls into two groups: calls Zoom flagged itself, and calls that were answered but lasted 5 seconds or less, which usually means a robocall, a silent line or a misdial. Spam is excluded from calls offered.
