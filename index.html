Answered vs missed

On weekdays ZimWorX answered 135 of 425 genuine calls and missed 290: 209 rolled to voicemail and 81 callers hung up before reaching it. A further 12 genuine calls came in on weekends and none were answered. Another 48 calls were spam.

Of the 55 calls answered between 19:00 and 01:00, 31 were picked up by Michelle and Rutendo after their shift ended. The overflow team answered 24 of the 239 calls in that window (10.0%). After 21:00, when Michelle and Rutendo stop picking up, the answer rate falls to 12.2% (17 of 139).

Opportunity: weekend coverage

The team does not work weekends, so every weekend caller goes unanswered. This year 12 genuine callers reached the line over the weekend, after the Friday overflow shift ended at 01:00 CAT, and all 12 went to voicemail.

12genuine weekend calls, 0 answered

Those callers are likely to go elsewhere: 82% of US consumers say they will call a competitor when a business does not answer (CallRail 2025, via Missed Call Statistics 2026). A short Saturday shift, or a missed-call text-back on weekends, would capture them.

About 1 in 10 calls is spam

Spam falls into two groups: calls Zoom flagged itself, and calls that were answered but lasted 5 seconds or less, which usually means a robocall, a silent line or a misdial.
